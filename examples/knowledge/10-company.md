# Synthetic example: Harbor Lane Studio is a fictional agency.

## WHO WE ARE
Harbor Lane Studio is a digital product agency. We design and build customer-facing apps, loyalty programmes and interactive campaigns for retail and consumer brands. A small team covers strategy, design, development and campaign operations in-house.

## HOW WE WORK
Every engagement starts with a short discovery workshop with the client's team. We agree on scope before we quote a price or a timeline. New products are launched as a pilot first and expanded only on measured results.

## WHAT WE DO NOT DO
We do not quote prices, deadlines or expected results in a first meeting. We do not comment on competitors or on other clients' work.
